
## Research Focus

Scanning Transmission Electron Microscopy (STEM) is an exceptionally powerful analytical technique capable of visualizing structures at the atomic scale. In recent years, significant advances in detector technology have enabled the acquisition of richer and more diverse information, leading to new developments in STEM methodologies. My research focuses on developing novel imaging techniques using state-of-the-art detectors, as well as applying these methods to address key challenges in materials science.

Current research topics include:

- Development of sensitive STEM imaging technique and its application to beam-sensitive materials  
- High-resolution electromagnetic field imaging: technique development and applications  
- Imaging atomic vibrations (phonons): method development and applications  
- Automatic optimization of STEM imaging conditions  
- Atomic-scale structural analysis of grain boundaries in steel
